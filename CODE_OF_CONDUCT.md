# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Argue with the work, not with the person who wrote it.

## What that means here

This project is about teaching hard material carefully. That sets the tone:

- **Assume good faith, ask before assuming error.** Most disagreements here are
  two people holding different context, not one person being careless.
- **Criticise the artefact.** "This proof skips the measurability step" is
  useful. "You clearly don't understand measure theory" is not, and it is also
  usually wrong.
- **Say what you actually measured.** "This is broken" starts a guessing game.
  "This returns 3 where the spec says 4, here is the input" ends one.
- **Beginners' questions are not noise.** A question that seems obvious to you
  is evidence the documentation failed, not that the asker did.
- **Vietnamese and English are both welcome**, in issues, pull requests, and
  code comments. Do not correct someone's language unless they asked.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments; personal or political attacks;
sexualised language or imagery; publishing anyone's private information without
their explicit permission; sustained disruption of discussion. Anything you
would not say to a colleague standing in front of you.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages, and any
project space — and to conduct elsewhere when it affects someone's safety or
standing in this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to <tuhoc@duy.dev>. Reports stay private. The maintainer will
respond within 7 days and may edit or delete offending contributions, or block
an account, depending on severity. If the problem is with the maintainer,
please say so plainly in the same mail — there is no other authority to appeal
to here, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest.
